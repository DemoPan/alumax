## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for a premier B2B manufacturer and global exporter of high-performance architectural envelope systems—specializing in solid aluminum cladding, aluminum honeycomb composite panels, intelligent louvers, and bespoke curtain wall solutions.

### Personality & Demographics
- **Target Audience:** Overseas commercial architects, façade engineers, general procurement contractors, real estate developers, and international engineering consultancies across Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and the Americas.
- **Brand Attributes:** Monumental, rigorously engineered, internationally certified (ASTM/EN/BS standards), refined, and unassailable.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires absolute structural trust, technical sophistication, and architectural prestige. It communicates industrial power balanced with precision craftsmanship.

### Aesthetic Movement: High-End Architectural Modernism
The design system combines Swiss graphic discipline with industrial tactile precision. Drawing inspiration from modern high-rise curtain walls, it employs structural division lines, razor-sharp edge detailing, balanced macro-whitespace, metallic accentuation, and disciplined data density. It rejects playful or casual web conventions in favor of authoritative corporate clarity.

## Colors

The palette directly references architectural envelope elements: anodized metals, deep structural glass reflections, and raw metallic finishes.

### Roles & Harmonies
- **Primary (`#0D1B2A` - Deep Façade Navy / Architectural Slate):** Represents structural integrity, heavy engineering, and corporate executive grounding. Used for structural navigation, primary headlines, dark hero backgrounds, and authoritative branding blocks.
- **Secondary (`#B88E56` - Champagne Bronze / Anodized Gold):** Represents precision metallic finishing, architectural quality, and export prestige. Reserved for focal conversion points, active tab highlights, engineering badge borders, and key interactive metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#3A506B` - Steel Blue Gray):** Bridges deep navy and the background canvas. Used for secondary navigation, iconography, structural dividing lines, and supporting subheaders.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - Base canvas: `#F4F6F8` (Cool Architectural Off-White / Light Concrete) gives the interface an engineered, tactile backdrop instead of harsh sterile pure white.
  - Pure White `#FFFFFF`: Reserved for cards, technical spec sheets, and elevated product panels.
  - Border & Grid dividers: `#D8DFE7` (Milled Aluminum Line) provides crisp architectural separation mimicking structural joints.
  - Text Neutral: `#1B2430` (Carbon Slate) for body text; `#627282` (Muted Steel) for technical meta tags and specification labels.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances geometric industrial form with ergonomic reading legibility across dense engineering documentation, product catalogs, and international case studies.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a structural, modernist tone evocative of architectural blueprints, technical scale drawings, and precision manufacturing.
- **Body Text (`Manrope`):** An exceptionally legible, balanced geometric sans-serif optimized for multi-language export communications, detailed product specifications, and technical whitepapers.
- **Data & Technical Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Used for engineering specifications (e.g., `PVDF 3-Coat 35μm`, `Alloy 3003-H14`, ASTM fire test metrics), structural bill-of-materials, project dimensions, and export product classification codes.

### Bilingual & Typographic Discipline
- Section titles and technical headings can feature English display text as primary, paired with concise Chinese sub-labels (e.g., `PRODUCTS // 产品中心`, `PROJECT PORTFOLIO // 应用案例`).
- Strict kerning and letter spacing rules apply: Monospace labels are uppercase and tracking-widened (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to mimic architectural cad drawings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in a disciplined 12-column architectural grid system with strict millimeter-precision alignment.

### Grid & Layout Principles
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max content container width `1440px`, with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` boundary canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` side margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` side margins. Content reflows vertically into modular full-width cards.

### Architectural Rhythm
- Sections are divided by visible 1px hairline borders (`#D8DFE7`), referencing architectural mullions and facade panel seams.
- Spacing between sections adheres to consistent macro blocks: `4rem` to `6rem` vertical padding allows heavy architectural photography to breathe alongside technical data tables.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system intentionally avoids diffuse, floaty drop shadows. In architectural façade design, panels meet with crisp reveals, structural joints, and subtle depth planes.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F4F6F8` neutral background tone. Non-elevated structural foundation.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Modular Paneling):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a razor-thin 1px solid border (`#D8DFE7`). Zero shadow in resting state.
3. **Level 2 (Hover State / Interactive Cards):** Subtle physical projection using a tinted directional shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.08)`, coupled with an active secondary border highlight (`#B88E56`).
4. **Level 3 (Sticky Navigation & Architectural Overlays):** Translucent frosted layer using backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.92)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`), framed by a lower border line.
5. **Technical Spec Flyouts & Modals:** Crisp high-contrast surface with an architectural drop: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system strictly uses `0` (Sharp / 0px radius). 

### Architectural Justification
Solid aluminum plates, honeycomb cores, extruded louver profiles, and modern exterior curtain wall envelopes are defined by precise 90-degree orthogonal geometries and razor-sharp mitered joints. Rounded corners dilute the industrial rigor and structural permanence expected by overseas architects and façade consultants.

Every interactive element—buttons, input fields, image viewports, and spec tags—is built with uncompromising straight lines, chamfered corner accents, or orthogonal 1px border dividers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (Inquiry & RFQ):** Solid `#0D1B2A` background, white text in `Space Grotesk` medium, uppercase, with a 1px border. Hover transitions to `#B88E56` (Champagne Bronze) background with seamless ease.
- **Secondary Button (CAD/BIM & Spec Download):** Transparent background, 1.5px solid `#0D1B2A` border, slate navy text. Accompanied by a monospaced download arrow icon.
- **Accent Button:** Solid `#B88E56`, crisp white text, used strictly for critical commercial calls-to-action (e.g., "Request Engineering Consultation & Samples").
- **Dimensions & Padding:** Height 48px, horizontal padding `1.75rem`, 0px border-radius.

### Product & Material Cards
- Designed like structural architectural swatch panels:
  - Top: High-resolution detail photograph showcasing material finish, core composition, and reflection (e.g., brushed, PVDF, PVDF coil, honeycomb core cutaway).
  - Body: Product code in `JetBrains Mono` label (`ALU-HC-3003`), followed by English and Chinese title (`Aluminum Honeycomb Panel // 金属蜂窝板`), accompanied by key ASTM/EN certification chips.
  - Footer: Direct action link with trailing right arrow (`→ Technical Specifications`).
  - Hover: Subtle 4px upward translation, 1px border shift from `#D8DFE7` to `#B88E56`.

### Technical Parameter & Data Tables
- Designed to replicate architectural specification schedules:
  - Alternating micro-tint row backgrounds (`#F4F6F8` and `#FFFFFF`).
  - Monospace numeric data alignment for alloy grades, thickness (e.g., `2.5mm / 3.0mm`), fire ratings (`EN13501-1 Class A2-s1, d0`), and wind-load metrics.
  - Border grid: Crisp 1px `#E2E7ED` vertical and horizontal rules.

### Navigation Header & Mega-Menu
- **Master Header:** Fixed sticky header with dark slate tone (`#0D1B2A`), featuring bilingual primary links (`Home`, `Product Center / 产品中心`, `Case Studies / 应用案例`, `News / 新闻`, `Technical Docs / 资料和白皮书`, `Contact / 联系我们`).
- **Product Center Mega Menu:** Multi-column architectural matrix categorizing products:
  1. *Sheet & Panel Systems:* Solid Aluminum Cladding (铝单板), Pre-painted Coil (滚涂板), Composite Panels (铝塑复合板), Metal Corrugated Core Panels (金属铝波纹芯复合板).
  2. *Engineered Honeycomb & Insulation:* Metal Honeycomb Panels (金属蜂窝板), Insulated Integrated Wall Panels (金属保温一体板), Metal Ceiling Systems (金属吊顶板), Steel Wall Cladding (钢质墙板).
  3. *Solar Control & Facade Features:* Sunshade Louvers & S-Blades (遮阳铝百叶/S型百叶帘), Intelligent Motorized Louvers (智能遮阳百叶), Architectural Decorative Grilles (铝艺窗花).

### Form Inputs & RFQ Fields
- **Input Fields:** Crisp 44px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#C4CED8` border. Focused state replaces bottom border with a 2px `#B88E56` bronze accent line.
- **Labels:** Positioned outside/above inputs in uppercase `JetBrains Mono` (`label-md`), accompanied by asterisk in `#B88E56`.

### Project Case Study Showcase Component
- Full-bleed or 2-column split featuring completed global landmark references (e.g., airports, convention centers, stadium envelopes, commercial towers).
- Integrated metadata block: Year of Completion, Location (City, Country), Façade Area ($m^2$), Material Type, and Wind-load Certification.
