ALUMAX to Showcase Next-Generation Kinetic Solar Louvers and Ultra-Flat Solid Cladding at The Big 5 Dubai 2025

AX

ALUMAX Global Engineering Media Desk

Published on November 24, 2024

Share:

DUBAI, UAE — With rapid capital acceleration across mega-scale architectural developments throughout the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, and Qatar, ALUMAX Architectural has confirmed its flagship presence for The Big 5 Global Dubai 2025. Taking place from November 24 to 27, 2025 at the Dubai World Trade Centre (DWTC), the international showcase will be stationed at Stand SS2-D41 (Sheikh Saeed Hall 2).

The primary focus of this year's delegation centers directly on engineered building envelope systems tailor-made for harsh GCC meteorological conditions: intense solar ultraviolet exposure, surface temperatures climbing beyond 75°C, high-velocity sand abrasion, and cyclonic coastal wind pressures exceeding 6.0 kPa.

Dynamic Solar Tracking and Extreme Climate Testing

A central attraction at Booth SS2-D41 will be an operable, full-height kinetic facade mock-up featuring extruded structural aluminum aerofoils. Equipped with heavy-duty 316 marine-grade rotary shafts, PTFE bronze bushings, and internal IP67 actuators, each blade pivots smoothly in synchrony with simulated solar radiation shifts.

Building simulation trials demonstrate that integrating ALUMAX automated solar tracking assemblies lowers direct solar heat gain coefficients (SHGC) to below 0.16 during peak irradiance at solar noon. For mega-towers in Riyadh and Dubai, this directly translates into an estimated 48% reduction in chiller plant cooling loads while preserving balanced interior daylight autonomy.

Hyperbolic Solid Aluminum Cladding for High-Rise Landmarks

In addition to motorized solar shading, ALUMAX will showcase its industry-leading hyperbolic and 3D double-curved solid aluminum cassette production lines. Manufactured using aerospace-grade alloy AA5052-H32 through precision multi-axis stretch forming, these cassettes achieve architectural tolerances within ±0.5mm while eliminating oil-canning and surface waviness.

Unlike aluminum composite panels (ACP) with organic cores that present disposal complications and combustibility hazards, ALUMAX solid aluminum cassettes are rated Class A1 non-combustible according to EN 13501-1 and the UAE Fire Code. Furthermore, the material is 100% recyclable indefinitely, fully supporting sustainability goals under UAE Net Zero 2050 and the Saudi Green Initiative.

GCC Delegation & Technical Consultations

Senior structural curtain wall engineers and international export project managers from ALUMAX's Dubai and Foshan engineering divisions will be stationed at Stand SS2-D41 throughout the four-day summit. Visiting facade consultants, master developers, and Tier-1 general contractors can review architectural specification schedules, obtain certified Revit BIM libraries, and receive complimentary custom sample specimens.
