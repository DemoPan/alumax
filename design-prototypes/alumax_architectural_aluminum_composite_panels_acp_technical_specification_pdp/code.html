Panel Fabrication & Substructure Jointing Systems

ALUMAX composite panels support rapid site routing, cassette returns, and cold roll-forming with conventional CNC or handheld grooving equipment without micro-fracturing the outer coating.

01 // MILLING 90° & 135° GROOVE

V-Groove CNC Milling for Sharp Corners

Route a 90° or 135° V-groove on the rear face leaving 0.5mm to 0.8mm of mineral core intact alongside the outer aluminum skin. This facilitates hand-folding into sharp crisp architectural cassettes with zero paint crazing.

FEED SPEED: 15–25 m/min
CUTTER TIP: R1.5mm to R2.0mm radius
02 // COLD BENDING MIN RADIUS 300MM

Roll-Bending for Columns & Soffits

Achieve continuous architectural curves using precision 3-roller pyramid bending machines. The elastomeric polymeric adhesive prevents core-skin shear delamination at cold radii as tight as R ≥ 300mm on 4.0mm panels.

MIN CURVATURE: R = 300mm (4mm panel)
ROLLER TYPE: Polished or Urethane Coated
03 // FIXING THERMAL REVEAL JOINT

Cassette Hanging & Top-Hat Substructure

Fix routed folded cassettes to aluminum alloy extruded subframes (6063-T6). Use slotted holes for thermal expansion allowance and concealed rivets or structural silicone for continuous pristine exterior reveals.

JOINT WIDTH: 15mm – 20mm Reveal
SEALANT: Neutral Silicone / Open Rain-screen
