waves

Sinusoidal Geometry Principle

The patented 12.5mm wave-pitch sinusoidal core acts as an infinite series of structural I-beams. Forces applied to the outer sheet are immediately converted into tensile and compressive vectors along the continuous flutes.

Flute Height: 2.8mm ± 0.05mm
air

Internal Micro-Cavity Ventilation

Unlike closed honeycomb cells, the open sinusoidal wave channels permit linear micro-aeration along the flute orientation. This prevents internal hydrostatic pressure spikes and accelerates thermal dissipation in desert climates.

Flow Area: 1,420 mm²/m Panel
recycling

Circular Mono-Material Stream

100% aluminum composition requires zero delamination sorting during building decommissioning. Scraps and retired cassettes are melted directly into secondary ingot stock with less than 5% original smelting energy.

Recycled Content: > 35% Post-Industrial

Standard Milling Profiles & Cassette Assembly

Engineered for CNC V-groove routing using standard tungsten carbide 90° or 135° cutters. Panels fold seamlessly into rigid architectural cassettes without fracturing the outer roll-coated skin.

DETAIL 01 Routing Specification

V-Groove 90° Routing & Flange Return

Routed from the backside through the rear skin and corrugated wave core. A calibrated 0.2mm - 0.3mm core base remains undisturbed to prevent metal fatigue when cold-folding 90° returns.

Minimum Bend Radius R = 2.0 mm
DETAIL 02 Subframe Integration

Concealed Fastening Cassette (Z-Clip)

Cassette folded flanges are riveted with structural corner brackets. Panels hook onto continuous vertical aluminum T- or Z-runners with stainless steel isolating washers to prevent bimetallic corrosion.
