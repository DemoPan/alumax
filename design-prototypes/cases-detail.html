Surviving 52°C Ambient Thermal Shock & Severe Desert Sand Abrasion

Riyadh's desert microclimate presents one of the most punishing structural envelope testing grounds globally, demanding multi-layered structural and chemical defenses against heat, UV, and particulate erosion.

01

Extreme Thermal Shock (+52°C Desert Microclimate & UV Degradation)

Thermal cycling causes intense differential expansion between glass substructures and aluminum mullions. Engineered expansion joints and continuous EPDM isolating thermal bridges absorb cyclic expansion without oil-canning or fastener fatigue.

02

Kinetic Dual-Skin BMS Automation Integration

ALUMAX synchronized motorized kinetic louvers into KAFD's central automation bus. Intelligent sensors dynamically trim aerofoil blade deflection in 0.5° increments based on solar azimuth, cutting peak solar thermal gain by 34.2%.

03

Hyperbolic Sandstorm Abrasion & Micro-Scratch Resistance

High-velocity particulate silica winds erode standard coil coatings. We engineered a bespoke 3-coat PPG Duranar XL fluoropolymer formulation loaded with ceramic microspheres, sustaining gloss and zero blistering past 4,000 hours of cyclic ASTM testing.
